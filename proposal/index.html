A Proposal for Curtis Brown Ltd

Stewardship for the Next Chapter

For nearly a century, Curtis Brown has represented authors whose words have shaped culture. A digital presence should honor that legacy while serving the expectations of readers, writers, and partners today.

This proposal outlines a partnership grounded in respect for what you have built and clarity about what comes next. Not a redesign for its own sake, but a thoughtful translation of institutional authority into a modern, maintainable digital experience.

Prepared by
Edgar Zorrilla and Paul Denya

Understanding the Moment

The digital landscape has shifted. What once felt current now signals age. What once worked seamlessly now creates friction.

Expectations Have Evolved

Authors researching representation, editors seeking submissions guidelines, international partners evaluating collaboration, readers discovering new voices they have encountered through social media or press coverage. Each arrives with expectations shaped by the best digital experiences they encounter elsewhere.

This does not mean chasing trends. It means meeting reasonable standards: clarity on mobile devices, fast load times, accessible navigation, and content that can be found when searched.

The Risk of Standing Still

An outdated digital presence does not merely look old. It creates operational friction: support inquiries that could be self-served, submission processes that create unnecessary back-and-forth, information that is difficult to maintain and therefore falls out of date.

More subtly, it creates a gap between the quality of your representation and the quality of your presentation. For an institution built on editorial judgment, that gap matters.

The Opportunity

A considered redesign is not a disruption. It is an opportunity to clarify what Curtis Brown is today, to streamline how you communicate with the world, and to build infrastructure that serves the agency for years to come, not just the current moment.

Why a Strategic Web Agency

Not every project requires scale. Some require depth, judgment, and the kind of attention that only comes from senior practitioners who remain involved from first conversation to final delivery.

What We Are

  • A two-person senior partnership
  • Twenty-plus years of combined experience in design, strategy, and engineering
  • Directly involved in every decision and deliverable
  • Focused on clarity, trust, and long-term value
  • Experienced with organizations where reputation and discretion matter

What We Are Not

  • Not a vendor managing offshore teams
  • Not a platform selling subscriptions
  • Not a startup testing ideas on your timeline
  • Not an agency that will hand you off to junior staff
  • Not interested in novelty for its own sake

Why This Matters for Curtis Brown

Literary agencies operate on trust. Authors trust you with their careers. Publishers trust your judgment. That trust is built through relationships, not transactions.

The same principle applies to digital work. A website is not a commodity to be purchased from the lowest bidder. It is an extension of your voice, your values, and your operational infrastructure. It deserves the same care you bring to representing an author.

We offer that care. Not because we are larger or faster or cheaper, but because we are senior enough to understand what you need and small enough to deliver it with attention.

Our Approach

A deliberate process that respects institutional complexity while maintaining forward momentum. No phase is skipped. No shortcuts are taken.

01

Discovery

Before designing anything, we listen. Structured conversations with stakeholders across the organization: leadership, agents, support staff, and anyone whose work intersects with the website.

  • Stakeholder interviews and priorities mapping
  • Content audit and information architecture review
  • Technical assessment of current infrastructure
  • Competitive and peer landscape analysis
  • User journey documentation for key audiences

Outcome: A shared understanding of what needs to change, what should be preserved, and what success looks like.

02

Architecture

The structure beneath the surface. How content is organized, how pages relate to each other, how users find what they need, and how the system will accommodate future growth.

  • Site map and navigation structure
  • Content models and editorial workflow
  • URL strategy and permalink design
  • Technical architecture decisions
  • Integration requirements and boundaries

Outcome: A documented architecture that serves as the foundation for all design and development work.

03

Design

Visual design rooted in the architecture. Not decoration applied after the fact, but design that emerges from and reinforces the underlying structure.

  • Design system and component library
  • Typography, color, and visual language
  • Key page templates and layouts
  • Responsive behavior across devices
  • Interactive prototypes for review

Outcome: A complete visual design that can be reviewed, refined, and approved before development begins.

04

Implementation

Building the site with the same care applied to design. Clean code, semantic markup, performance optimization, and accessibility as foundational requirements rather than afterthoughts.

  • Front-end development with modern standards
  • Content management integration
  • Performance optimization and testing
  • Accessibility audit and remediation
  • Cross-browser and device testing

Outcome: A production-ready website that performs well and can be confidently launched.

05

Handoff

Delivery is not the end of our responsibility. We ensure your team can maintain, update, and extend what we have built without ongoing dependency on us.

  • Comprehensive documentation
  • Training for content editors and administrators
  • Launch support and monitoring
  • Post-launch optimization period
  • Clear ownership transfer

Outcome: Full ownership and capability to operate independently, with the option for ongoing support if desired.

Extensions That Support the Core

Beyond the public website, there are operational systems that naturally connect to and extend the core digital presence. These are available but not assumed. Each requires its own careful consideration.

Optional

Structured intake systems for manuscript submissions, query letters, and author inquiries. Designed to reduce administrative burden while ensuring nothing is lost and guidelines are followed.

Optional

Private interfaces for staff to manage website content, view submission pipelines, and access operational data without requiring technical expertise.

  • Submission queue and status tracking
  • Basic reporting and analytics views
  • User roles and permission management

Secure Document Handling

Optional

When sensitive materials must be exchanged digitally, purpose-built systems that prioritize security and auditability over convenience.

  • Encrypted file transfer
  • Time-limited access links
  • Download tracking and audit logs
  • No third-party cloud storage dependencies

Private AI Classification

Opt-In Only

For organizations processing high volumes of submissions, AI-assisted classification can help surface relevant materials faster. Available only with explicit consent and strict data boundaries.

  • Genre and category classification
  • Quality and completeness flagging
  • Runs on-premises or private cloud only
  • No data used for model training
  • Human review remains authoritative

We include this capability with significant caveats. AI should be an assistive tool for operational efficiency, never a replacement for editorial judgment. Implementation requires careful discussion of boundaries and expectations.

Security, Privacy, and Trust

A literary agency handles some of the most sensitive materials in publishing: unpublished manuscripts, contract negotiations, career-defining decisions. Digital systems that touch this work must be built with corresponding care.

Clear Data Boundaries

We establish explicit boundaries around what data exists, where it lives, and who can access it. Public website content is separate from submission systems. Submission systems are separate from internal operations. Nothing crosses boundaries without explicit design and approval.

Manuscript Protection

Unpublished work is irreplaceable intellectual property. Any system that handles manuscripts includes encryption at rest, encrypted transmission, access logging, and retention policies that ensure materials are not stored longer than necessary.

Ownership and Portability

You own your data, your content, and your systems. We build on open standards and avoid proprietary lock-in. If you choose to work with different partners in the future, everything we build transfers cleanly.

Long-Term Stability

We select technologies for durability, not novelty. Frameworks and platforms with established track records, active maintenance, and clear migration paths. Your website should work reliably for years, not require rebuilding when trends change.

No Unnecessary Dependencies

Every external service and third-party integration is evaluated for necessity and risk. We prefer fewer dependencies with clear purposes over feature-rich platforms that create exposure.

Our Commitment

We are not a security firm and do not position ourselves as one. But we understand that trust is foundational to your business, and we build systems that respect that trust. Where specialized security expertise is required, we recommend and coordinate with qualified professionals.
